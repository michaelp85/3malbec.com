I used 2 main methods to cost and budget our build:
* [Rawlinsons Construction Cost Guide](https://www.rawlhouse.com.au/publications/2026-construction-cost-guide)
* *Retail* pricing that I could find online

And as a general rule of thumb, I always budgeted for the high end with the theory being I'd rather be surprised that something came in cheaper, than be shocked at actually how expensive something is.

My overall budget for this build is $1m, but I am confident I can bring this in at the $700k mark (I type that, as we just complete our foundation stage). When I was pricing this out, I was originally quoted around $1.2 to 1.4m by local architects and custom builders in the Canberra region.

## Council & Pre-Start

| Item | Budget | Actual Spend | Notes |
| --- | -------: | ------------: | ---------------------------- |
| DA Fees | $10,000 | $12,256 | This came in higher due to [NSW Long Service Levy (Building and construction)](https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/transaction/pay-the-long-service-levy-building-and-construction) that I only found out about after our DA was approved. |
| Certifier | $4,000 | $2,970 | Test |
| Surveyor | $4,000 | $2,200 | Cash job brought this in much cheaper, also did several GCP targets for me for drone usage |
| Insurance | $10,000 | $6,659 | I initially budgeted this based on a pure guess |
