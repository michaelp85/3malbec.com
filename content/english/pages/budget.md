I used 3 main methods to cost and budget our build; I've will also be creating a detailed blog post about this topic.
* [Rawlinsons Construction Cost Guide](https://www.rawlhouse.com.au/publications/2026-construction-cost-guide)
* *Retail* and online pricing, plus my experience I have with technology supply chains
* Quote from our builder, broken down into 46 categories.

As a general rule, we've realistically budgeted this build — I'd rather be pleasantly surprised when something comes in cheaper than shocked by how expensive it actually turns out to be. I won't publically put our budget here.

The charts and data on this page will update nightly at <a href="https://github.com/michaelp85/3malbec.com/actions/workflows/update-balance.yml" target="_blank">10.42pm via a Github scheduled Action</a>, pulling data from <a href="https://www.pocketsmith.com/" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">Pocketsmith</a>.
