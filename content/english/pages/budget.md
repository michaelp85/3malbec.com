I used 2 main methods to cost and budget our build:
* [Rawlinsons Construction Cost Guide](https://www.rawlhouse.com.au/publications/2026-construction-cost-guide)
* *Retail* and online pricing, plus my experience I have with technology supply chains

As a general rule, I always budgeted for the high end — I'd rather be pleasantly surprised when something comes in cheaper than shocked by how expensive it actually turns out to be.
